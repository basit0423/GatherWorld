# Phase 13 — Food & Local Commerce: Search Discovery

Food listings now support restaurant-focused information: cuisine, menu highlights, opening hours, typical spend, contact phone, suitability notes, dietary/allergy/access notes and a source link. Public detail pages explain what visitors can check before deciding whether a restaurant suits them. Members can submit reviews; new reviews remain pending until admin moderation. Only approved reviews contribute to visible community ratings and Restaurant structured data.

Area pages use `/food/in/<location-slug>` and are added to the dynamic sitemap only when there are at least two sufficiently complete, sourced listings in that area. Incomplete detail pages remain accessible but use `noindex,follow` until they contain adequate information.

Gather World does not claim reviews represent verified visits, does not invent ratings and does not guarantee Google rankings or review-star displays. Menus, prices and hours must be checked with the provider.
