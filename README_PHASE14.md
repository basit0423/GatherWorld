# Phase 14 — Travel & Tourism: Search Discovery

Travel listings now support destination research fields: best season, suggested duration, how to get there, attractions, per-person daily budget range and currency, budget breakdown, sample itinerary, local tips, safety/access notes, contact phone and a source link. A page calculator estimates a group total from the supplied daily range, duration and group size; estimates are not live quotes.

Area pages use `/travel/in/<location-slug>` and are included in the dynamic sitemap only when there are at least two sufficiently complete, sourced entries in that area. Incomplete detail pages remain accessible but use `noindex,follow` until they contain adequate information.

Travel prices, road conditions, permits, weather and opening times change. Contributors should date/check their sources and visitors should confirm current details before travelling.
