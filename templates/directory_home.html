MAKE AN INFORMED CHOICE

How to decide whether a restaurant is worth visiting

Look beyond a star score. Compare the cuisine, menu highlights, typical cost, opening hours, location and the experiences described in community reviews. Check when the information was last updated and confirm today's menu, prices and availability with the restaurant before travelling.

What should I check?

Menu and portion information, published prices, dietary and allergen details, opening hours, contact information and the restaurant's own menu or website.

How do community reviews help?

Read several approved reviews, pay attention to their dates and details, and consider whether their priorities match yours. A small number of reviews is not a guarantee of quality.

Why compare more than one place?

Comparing cuisine, location, budget and facilities helps you find a restaurant that matches the occasion and your needs.

How to estimate the cost of a trip

A useful trip estimate separates transport, accommodation, food, local travel and activities. Gather destination pages show per-person daily budget ranges and sample breakdowns when contributors provide them. Treat estimates as planning guides, not live quotations.

What should a destination guide explain?

Where the place is, why people visit, its main attractions, best season, access routes, local facilities and practical visitor advice.

How should I compare budgets?

Compare the same trip length and travel style. Check whether transport to the destination, accommodation, meals, entry fees and local transfers are included.

Why check dates and sources?

Weather, road access, fares, room rates and attraction schedules change. Confirm current conditions with official providers before making plans.
