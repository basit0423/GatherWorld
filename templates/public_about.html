ABOUT GATHER WORLD

One world. One platform. Endless possibilities.

Gather World is being built as a welcoming place to connect, share ideas, discover communities and explore useful digital experiences.

What you can do

Members can create profiles, publish text and media posts, follow people, join communities, send messages, save content and discover public conversations.

Our longer-term vision includes shopping discovery, creator tools, business directories, learning, entertainment and other useful services. Features will be introduced gradually and clearly labeled as they become available.

Our approach

We aim to keep Gather World simple to use, respectful, accessible on mobile and desktop, and transparent about how features and partner services work.
