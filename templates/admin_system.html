Manual release checklist

  • Confirm a recent Neon backup and test restoring it to a separate database.
  • Verify domain DNS, HTTPS certificate, canonical URL and environment variables at the hosting provider.
  • Run browser tests for registration, OTP, reset, uploads, moderation, messages, communities, AI, creators and business pages.
  • Review privacy policy, terms, content rules, retention and data deletion procedures.
  • Review access controls, rate limits, upload limits and dependency updates.
  • Set APP_ENV=production, FLASK_DEBUG=0, a strong FLASK_SECRET_KEY and COOKIE_SECURE=1 for HTTPS hosting.

Do not treat this dashboard as a penetration test, legal review, backup restore test or launch approval.
