Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026. This draft explains the main information Gather World handles during local development and testing. It must be reviewed and updated before public launch.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, Gather World may process your username, email address, display name, profile details, password hash, posts, comments, follows, saved items, messages, community activity and support requests.

Media and service providers

When configured, uploaded profile images and post media are stored using Cloudinary. Account verification and password-reset messages use the configured email provider. The database is hosted by Neon. When you use Gather AI, your prompt and relevant conversation context are sent to Google's Gemini service to generate a response.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, show content, deliver notifications, protect the service, respond to support requests and provide features you choose to use.

Public content

Public posts, public profiles and public community activity may be visible to other visitors. Do not publish information you intend to keep private.

External websites

Gather World may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices, checkout processes and account settings are controlled by those providers, not Gather World.

Choices and requests

For privacy questions or requests about your account information, contact support@gathercommunity.me. Operational retention, deletion and backup procedures must be finalized before public launch.
