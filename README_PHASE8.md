GATHER WORLD — PHASE 8: CREATOR & BUSINESS ECOSYSTEM

Adds public creator profiles, a creator directory, a creator dashboard with basic follower/post/profile-view counts, business pages, business discovery, owner editing and business follows.

Routes: /creators, /creator/settings, /creator/dashboard, /creator/<username>, /businesses, /businesses/create, /business/<slug>, /business/<slug>/edit.

Business listings start as unverified. Gather World does not guarantee the identity or quality of a listed organization. The feature is a directory/presence layer only: it does not process orders, payments, seller payouts, bookings or affiliate commissions. Those are separate later phases and need partner agreements and payment-provider review.
