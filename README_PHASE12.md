PHASE 12 — GATHER SERVICES & FREELANCING
----------------------------------------
- Public service directory with search and category filters.
- Members can create service/provider listings with descriptions, rate labels,
  location, contact email and HTTPS portfolio/website URLs.
- Listing owners can edit, hide and restore their entries.
- Interested members can send a private service inquiry; the provider receives
  an in-app notification and can read inquiries from My Listings.
- No payment escrow, checkout, guarantees, or dispute resolution is included.
